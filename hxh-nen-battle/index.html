Nen Card Battle

Magic-style combat with Aura, Characters, Techniques and full Nen affinities. Build a deck and face AI Hunters.

How to Play

Objective

Reduce opponent life from 20 to 0.

Aura

Max Aura +1 each turn and refills fully. Spend it to play cards.

Turn

  • Draw → Main (play cards) → Combat (attackers / blockers) → End

Targeting

When you play a Technique that needs a target, valid targets glow gold. Click the enemy character or the AI life total (face) to choose.

  • Transmutation Tricks & elements
