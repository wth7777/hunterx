Hunter × Hunter
Nen Card Battle

Magic: The Gathering style combat with Aura resources, Character cards, Technique spells, and full Nen affinities. Build your deck and face AI Hunters of increasing difficulty.

How to Play

Objective

Reduce your opponent’s life from 20 to 0. You control Characters (creatures) and Techniques (instant/sorcery effects).

Aura (Resources)

At the start of each of your turns your maximum Aura increases by 1 and you gain that full amount. Spend Aura to play cards. Unused Aura does not carry over.

Turn Structure

  • Draw – Draw 1 card.
  • Main – Play Characters and Techniques (pay their Aura cost).
  • Combat – Choose attackers. Opponent chooses blockers. Damage is dealt simultaneously.
  • End – Turn passes to opponent.

Nen Affinities

  • Enhancement Heavy attacks & durability
  • Transmutation Elemental tricks & flexibility
  • Emission Ranged attacks
  • Conjuration Weapons, chains & constructs
  • Manipulation Control & disabling
  • Specialization Unique rule-bending powers

Fundamentals

Ten (defense), Zetsu (stealth/hide), Ren (power-up), Hatsu (signature ability) inspire many card effects.

Combat

Attackers deal their power as damage. Blockers assign damage to attackers. Excess damage from unblocked attackers hits the opponent’s life. Characters with 0 or less toughness die.
